1. Scope & Formulation

Problem Statement & Phase 1 Scope

Phase 1 Challenge

  • Accurate classification of recyclable waste into 6 material categories: cardboard, glass, metal, paper, plastic, trash.
  • Strict Constraint: Classical machine learning only (no deep transfer learning or neural networks).
  • High Material Ambiguity: Transparent glass vs. plastic; metallic foil vs. cans; crumpled paper vs. cardboard.

Key Architectural Principles

1. Strict Zero-Leakage: Audited duplicate grouping across splits and cross-validation folds.

2. Interpretable Descriptors: Multi-color space histograms, gradient orientation (HOG), and local textures (LBP).

3. Calibrated Uncertainty: Selective classification to abstain from ambiguous decisions before human inspection.

3. Feature Engineering

Handcrafted Visual Representations

Baseline Descriptors (1,806 Dims)

1. HSV Color Histograms (32 dims): 16 Hue, 8 Saturation, 8 Value bins; per-channel L1-normalized.

2. HOG (1,764 dims): 9 signed orientation bins, 16×16 px/cell, 2×2 blocks, Dalal-Triggs L2-Hys block clipping.

3. Uniform LBP (10 dims): Circular P=8, R=1.0 uniform patterns, L1-normalized.

Extended Spatial Descriptors (2,760 Dims)

Multi-Region Decomposition: 6 regions (global, foreground, 4 spatial quadrants) on 256×256 canvas.

Rich Color & Texture: HSV, RGB, LAB, joint HS (288 dims) + multi-scale LBP (R∈{1,2,3}, 172 dims) per region.

Winner Selection: 1,608 dims (global color + spatial texture).

5. Theoretical Insights

Key Insights: SVM vs. Random Forest

Random Forest Subspace Dilution

• Baseline RF dropped from 0.7366 (color only) to 0.7090 (all features).

• Cause: Random feature sampling at split nodes ($m = \sqrt{1806} \approx 42$) diluted color candidates to only 1.8% of the pool.

• Resolution: Tuning max_features=0.25 on extended spatial representations lifted RF to 0.8370 ± 0.0241.

RBF SVM Nonlinear Separation

• RBF kernel maps features into an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space.

• Elementwise square root + L2 normalization (root_l2) effectively linearizes Bhattacharyya histogram distances.

• Achieved global winner CV score: 0.8803 ± 0.0271 ($C=10.0, \gamma=10.0$).

6. Calibration & Abstention

Platt Scaling & Validation-Only Threshold

Group-Aware Sigmoid Calibration

  • Trained via CalibratedClassifierCV(ensemble=True) over the 5 training folds.
  • Base estimators fit on 4/5 training complement; Platt sigmoid fits on 1/5 withheld fold.
  • Guarantees: Zero calibration on training data.

Threshold Selection (τ = 0.52)

• Swept 101 thresholds on 380 validation images.

• Target: Accepted accuracy ≥ 90%, coverage ≥ 80%.

• Selected Threshold: τ = 0.52

• Validation Coverage: 98.16% (373/380)

• Validation Accepted Accuracy: 90.08%

Interactive Gradio Demo & Safety UI

Implementation (app.py)

  • Local Privacy: Public sharing disabled (share=False); photos not stored in project directories (transient OS temp storage used by Gradio).

Uncertainty UX & Safety

• Prominent Warning: When confidence < 0.52, primary output becomes "Uncertain — manual inspection recommended."

• Full Probability Table: All 6 class probabilities formatted clearly.

• Limitations Banner: Explicitly warns that confidence does not detect unknown out-of-distribution items.
